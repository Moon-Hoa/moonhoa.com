## Currency — Oxygen Credits (OC)

Oxygen Credits are the only currency used anywhere on the site. Never use
USD alone, "moon dollars," or the `₸` symbol — both were placeholder
content from the pre-Rev.9 build.

Where a real-dollar equivalent is useful, show both, using the
Association's own quarterly exchange rate (Appendix T):

> "200 OC (≈ $153 at this quarter's Association-set rate, Appendix T)"

Appendix T's most recent annual snapshot (indexed, OC per Earth-currency
unit): 2088 = 1.00 (base, at incorporation) → 2089 = 1.04 → 2090 = 1.11 →
2091 = 1.22 → 2092 = 1.19 → 2093 = 1.25 → **2094 = 1.31 (current, as of
this printing)**. The rate has been unfavourable for as long as records
have been kept, per §1.4 — don't "fix" this into a favorable number.

## Timeline / "what year is it"

The Charter's own internal chronology — 2086 Great Regolith Incident →
2088 incorporation → 2094 "as of this printing" — is lore, not the site's
live present. Dynamic, per-visit content (registration, dues invoices,
live meeting minutes if they become dynamic) should not print a hard
current year. Static archival content (past meeting minutes, the
Historical Incident Log, resolutions) legitimately carries real
in-universe dates and should stay anchored to 2088+.

## Citation & naming conventions

- Cite the Charter directly: `§[Part].[Section]` (e.g. `§3.2.2`). There
  is no "LCID Act" or any other invented external statute — Rev.9 is
  self-contained. The one real exception is the Charter-native Lunar
  Heritage Preservation Accord (§9.8), a real multilateral framework
  referenced within the Charter itself.
- Resolutions: `Resolution [YYYY]-R-[##]`, anchored to 2088+ (matches the
  Charter's own `2089-R-01`, `2091-R-14`, `2094-R-09`, etc.).
- Tribunal cases: `Case No. T-[YYYY]-[###]`, anchored to 2088+.
- Forms: use real Charter form numbers (`LRA-300-STR` Structural
  Approval, `LRA-042-A` Residency Application, `LRA-199` Transfer
  Notice, `LRA-COMPLAINT-1` Neighbour Complaint, `LRA-VIO-1` Violation
  Notice, `LRA-905` Volumes II–XII Request, `LRA-514-#####` Records
  Request ticket format) rather than inventing new ones. Full directory
  at Appendix A of the Charter.
- **Part VIII does not exist.** The real Charter goes straight from Part
  VII (Amenities, Events & Community Life) to Part IX (Legacy Equipment)
  with zero explanation anywhere in the 109-page document. This is worth
  preserving as one of the Charter's own unresolved oddities — do not
  invent a Part VIII or renumber around the gap.

## Running mysteries & gags — preserve and extend, don't resolve

- **The missing Zero-Gravity Swimming Pool.** Reserve Component funded at
  11% (critical shortfall) since the first Reserve Study (2091), a status
  that "has not materially improved since." Last known heading/location:
  unknown, per the current Special Assessment Notice. Ties to `/reserve-study`
  and `/special-assessment`.
- **Keeper of the Dust Drift Ledger, vacant since 2092.** An unsigned
  resignation note was found pinned to the Ledger itself. Unconfirmed
  sightings have been logged at Full Moon Parties (most recently 2093 —
  the individual "declined to comment and left before the seating chart
  could be consulted"). The Legacy Equipment Preservation Subcommittee
  has twice proposed merging the seat into its own membership; both
  proposals lapsed for lack of a Keeper to consult about their own
  merger.
- **"MONS RULE" graffiti.** Three confirmed sightings: 2090, 2092, 2093
  (the third accompanied by a second, shorter inscription the Board has
  declined to reproduce in the Historical Incident Log). Investigation
  remains open. Anonymous tips are the one explicit exception to the
  Charter's no-anonymous-complaints rule (§14.2.1) and are "actively
  encouraged."
- **The cipher.** A number sequence — `23-5-11-14-15-23` — was found
  stamped in the margin of the final Ledger page in 2092, filed as "a
  printing artifact... not otherwise explained." A=1...Z=26 decodes it to
  **WE KNOW**. Do not solve this in site copy. It's a deliberate seed for
  a future puzzle/ARG-style feature, not a bug to fix.
- **The Appendix B / §6.2 fine discrepancy.** Unauthorised surface
  writing is listed at 75 OC in the Appendix B master schedule and 50 OC
  in §6.2. The Charter itself acknowledges this ("either a clerical
  error or a test. It has not decided which.") — never silently
  reconcile the two numbers.
